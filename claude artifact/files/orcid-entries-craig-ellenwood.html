How to Add These to Your ORCID

  1. Go to orcid.org/signin and log in
  2. Scroll to the Professional Activities section
  3. Click + Add next to the appropriate category (Service, Distinction, or Invited Position)
  4. Copy each field below into the corresponding form field
  5. Click each highlighted field to copy it to your clipboard
  6. Add the URL in the URL field to link directly to the published work
  7. Save — ORCID will verify the URL and create a permanent linked record
